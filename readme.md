# Enhancing Monetary Velocity in French Polynesia

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes French Polynesia's economy through monetary velocity—how quickly money circulates through the economic system. With a 20% decline in velocity since 2019 and no ability to issue currency, innovative fiscal strategies are essential for economic growth.

## Key Highlights

- **Problem:** Monetary velocity down ~20% since 2019
- **Solution:** Eight targeted fiscal initiatives
- **Investment:** 3.1 billion XPF net budget increase
- **Potential Impact:** 15-25% velocity increase over 2-3 years
- **Economic Gain:** 80-130 billion XPF in additional annual activity
